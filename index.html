What is Qissa?

A word that means a tale.

Food is all about its story — the origin, the spices, the texture and the taste of every dish. It is the cornerstone of daily life, of culture, of history.

‘Qissa’ tells the extraordinary stories behind the foods we eat: the finest Indian cuisine, served with careful attention to every detail of food, service and ambience.

Qissa is a place of gathering, of celebrations, of business, and of pleasure. Let the story begin… — The Qissa Promise
